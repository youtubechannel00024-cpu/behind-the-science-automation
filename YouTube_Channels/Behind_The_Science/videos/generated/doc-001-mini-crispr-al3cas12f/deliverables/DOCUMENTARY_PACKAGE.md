## 2. Scientific Research Verification & Peer-Reviewed Reference List

- **Exact Identity:** **Al3Cas12f** (metagenomic designation **MG119-28**), an RNA-guided Class 2 **Type V-F** miniature CRISPR nuclease discovered in *Alistipes* sp.
- **Primary Peer-Reviewed Study:**
  - **Guan, K., Taylor, D. W., Finkelstein, I. J., et al. (April 2026).** *"Comparative characterization of Cas12f orthologs reveals mechanistic features underlying enhanced genome editing efficiency."* ***Nature Structural & Molecular Biology***. Collaborators: **University of Texas at Austin** & **Metagenomi** (NIH-supported research).
- **Verified Molecular Size Comparison:**
  - ***Sp*Cas9 (Classic):** `1,368 amino acids` (`~4.1–4.2 kb` coding gene)
  - **Cas12a:** `~1,228 amino acids`
  - **Un1Cas12f1:** `529 amino acids`
  - **Al3Cas12f (MG119-28):** `~433–488 amino acids` (`~1/3` the molecular weight/size of *Sp*Cas9)
- **Verified Molecular Mechanism:**
  1. **Asymmetric Homodimer Assembly:** Two monomers of `Al3Cas12f` form a pre-assembled asymmetric bilobed homodimer stabilized around a single guide RNA (`gRNA`), avoiding the weak folding seen in earlier compact Cas12f orthologs.
  2. **PAM Check:** Recognizes T-rich `TTTR` PAM checkpoints validated across loci including *AAVS1*, *APOA1*, and Albumin intron 1.
  3. **R-Loop Unwinding & RuvC Cut:** Unwinds the target dsDNA duplex to match the guide RNA spacer, activating the **RuvC** nuclease catalytic domain for a double-stranded break (DSB).
  4. **Engineered Variant (`Al3Cas12f RKK`):** Rational cryo-EM interface/DNA-binding engineering boosted editing efficiency in human cells (*in vitro* `HEK293T`, challenging genomic loci linked to ALS, atherosclerosis, and cancer pathways) from **<10% baseline to 80–90%+**.
  5. **Cutting ≠ Repairing:** CRISPR cuts the target DNA; **endogenous cellular DNA repair pathways** perform actual healing via **NHEJ** (Non-Homologous End Joining — rapid patching that can disrupt harmful genes via indels) or **HDR** (Homology-Directed Repair — template-guided precise repair, typically low frequency *in vivo*).
- **Delivery Advantage & Scientific Reality Check:**
  - Single-stranded **AAV** (*Adeno-Associated Virus*) vectors have a strict packaging limit of **`~4.7 kb`**. *Sp*Cas9 (`~4.2 kb` alone) leaves insufficient room for promoters, terminators, and `gRNA`, often forcing dual-vector or *ex-vivo* workflows. `Al3Cas12f` fits cleanly inside one all-in-one AAV vector alongside its `gRNA` and regulatory switches.
  - **Clinical Stage:** Strictly **in vitro human cell line & cryo-EM biochemical research**. NOT in human clinical trials and NOT an approved medical treatment.
